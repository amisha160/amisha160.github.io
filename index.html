AI Driver Risk Monitor

A driver-safety platform that classifies driving behaviour in real time as Safe, Medium or High risk, and combines the signals into one risk score.

  • Logistic Regression classifier built with Scikit-learn
  • Voice alerts, driver image analysis, trip history and CSV export
  • Deployed on Render, with an IEEE-style research paper in draft

Why it matters: it turns behaviour signals into timely, understandable feedback for a real driver.

Built with Python, Scikit-learn, Pandas, NumPy, HTML, CSS. Team project with K S Chandana, guided by Vishwanath R Hulipalled.

Explainable AI for Indian Skin Cancer

A deep-learning framework for early melanoma and skin-cancer detection on Indian skin types, which most public datasets under-represent.

  • Combines clinical and dermoscopic images with lesion segmentation and multimodal fusion
  • Explains its predictions and reports uncertainty
  • Checks fairness and calibration across skin types, with a lightweight mobile prototype planned

Why it matters: healthcare AI has to be explainable, fair across groups and honest about what it doesn't know. This project is where I practise that.

Setting REVA University's Center for AI & ML. Team project with Harshitha K M, guided by Dr Gururaj V Murtugudde.
